# 3-minute demo script (screen-record with Loom)

Practice once, then record. Keep to ~2:45.

**0:00 - Intro (15s)**
"This is LeadPilot. A rep with hundreds of leads a day needs to know who matters, what they want, and what to do next. Let me show the flow."

**0:15 - Add a lead (35s)**
Click "+ New lead", pick *Sample: ready buyer* (Priya). Click "Analyze with AI". While it loads: "The form data goes to a FastAPI backend, which calls Llama 3.3 on Groq in JSON mode."

**0:50 - Read the output (35s)**
Point at: next-action banner, score + HOT badge, summary/intent, requirements chips, objections (possession delay), suggested reply. "A rep can scan this in a few seconds. The reply is editable and has a copy button."

**1:25 - Prioritization (25s)**
Add *Sample: just browsing* quickly (or have it pre-added). Show sidebar: hot at top, cold below, follow-up due badges, and the "follow-ups due" banner.

**1:50 - Conversational copilot (25s)**
Open Priya, click "What should I emphasize on the call?". Then type "make my reply more assertive". "It's grounded: it quotes her loan pre-approval and the November deadline - it's not a generic chatbot."

**2:15 - My own feature (30s)**
"Log a call" tab. Paste: "Husband liked the unit but wants 2% off. Will visit Sunday if parking is covered." Click update. Show the score delta, "what changed", the new next action, and the timeline tab. "Real work happens before, during and after the call, so the lead re-scores itself after each interaction."

**2:45 - One decision to discuss (15s)**
"One decision: the AI produces the score but the hot/warm/cold tier is computed in code from thresholds, so ranking is deterministic and tunable. Happy to go deeper in the interview."
